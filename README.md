# City Commodities Group Website

Premium corporate website redesign for City Commodities (Pvt) Ltd, Sri Lanka.

## Product direction

The website should present City Commodities as an established, modern Sri Lankan group spanning global sourcing, wholesale/distribution, retail, and bakery/cake supplies. It must feel premium, trustworthy and commercially strong rather than like a generic grocery template.

## Brand system

- Primary typography: Poppins.
- Core surfaces: deep graphite / charcoal, approximately #111111 and #171717 rather than pure black.
- Primary accent: City orange #F15D22 with #FF8200 as the brighter orange accent.
- Supporting surfaces: warm off-white #F5F5F3 and white #FFFFFF.
- Use orange deliberately for CTAs, small labels, rules, key numbers and interaction states. Avoid flooding sections with orange.
- Large clean typography, strong grid, generous whitespace, premium commercial photography and restrained motion.
- Avoid generic AI-site styling: no glowing blobs, excessive gradients, glassmorphism everywhere, excessive rounded cards, repetitive three-column card grids, random stock illustrations, heavy shadows or clutter.

## Homepage direction

1. Premium navigation with City Commodities Group identity and concise navigation.
2. Full-height cinematic hero: “Built on Trust. Driven by Quality.” Position the group around global sourcing, wholesale distribution, retail and specialty products across Sri Lanka. Include Explore Our Businesses and Contact Us actions plus compact trust indicators.
3. Group introduction: “One Group. Multiple Businesses. One Standard of Excellence.” with concise copy and editable statistics.
4. Our Businesses: strong photographic presentations for City Logistics, City Super and City Essence. These should feel editorial and immersive rather than like small generic cards.
5. Global sourcing: dark world-map-inspired visual area with orange points and concise international sourcing story.
6. Product/category showcase for Rice & Grains, Pulses & Essentials, Dates & Dry Foods, Bakery Ingredients, FMCG and Retail Products. Images must be easy to replace without redesigning layouts.
7. Supply-chain story: Source → Import → Store → Distribute, covering sourcing, quality assurance, warehousing/logistics and island-wide distribution.
8. Brand-value photographic statement: “From Global Markets to Sri Lankan Businesses.”
9. Why City Commodities: numbered editorial layout for global sourcing, supplier network, competitive pricing, reliable logistics, diverse portfolio and customer-focused service.
10. Premium closing CTA: “Let’s Build Better Business Together.”
11. Clean corporate footer with group businesses, navigation, contact placeholders and social links.

## Engineering requirements

- Responsive desktop, tablet and mobile experience.
- Reusable components and a maintainable design system.
- Keep business copy, statistics, contact data, images, category data and brand panels easy to update from centralized content/data structures instead of scattering content through components.
- Use replaceable image slots with sensible focal-point handling and overlays so photography can change without breaking text readability.
- Semantic HTML and accessible keyboard/focus behavior.
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion.
- Optimize images and performance.
- SEO-ready metadata and heading hierarchy.
- Keep animations subtle: reveal, controlled image scale, small line/number movement; never distract from content.

## Content note

Do not copy irrelevant template material from the legacy site. In particular, generic organic-farm/organic-food content should not be migrated. Corporate content should accurately describe City Commodities and its actual businesses.

## Build strategy

Establish the homepage and core design system first. After visual approval, extend the same system to About, Our Businesses, City Logistics, City Super, City Essence, Products/Brands, Insights and Contact.
